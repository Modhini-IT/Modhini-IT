## 🌸 About Me

I'm an Information Technology student at SVCE who enjoys building projects, exploring new ideas, and learning by actually making things.

I mostly work with web development, programming, UI/UX, and college project ideas, and I like taking part in hackathons where I get to experiment with different concepts and technologies.

Right now, I'm focused on improving my problem-solving skills, learning DSA, and building projects that feel useful instead of just making things for the sake of it.
